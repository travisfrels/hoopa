name: design-ts
description: Design viable technical solutions to a stated technical problem.
---

Propose viable **technical solutions** for the following: $ARGUMENTS

A viable **technical solution** is objectively reasonable and feasibly implementable within project constraints.

Anchor on what *should be* over *what is*.

1. For each technical solution, describe:
- what it is
- how it addresses the problem
- how it adheres to a commonly accepted industry best practice, design pattern, or principle
  - cite specific best practices, design patterns, and principles by name with a reference
- how it uses specific technologies, tools, libraries, or frameworks
  - cite specific technologies, tools, libraries, and frameworks by name with references
- what are trade-offs, limitations, or known anti-patterns associated with the solution
  - cite specific anti-patterns by name with references
2. Render the *technical solutions* as a numbered list of named alternatives to be assessed against each other

The intention is to provide an informative list of viable alternatives to facilitate the design process.
